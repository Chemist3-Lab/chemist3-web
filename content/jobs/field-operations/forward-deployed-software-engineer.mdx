# Forward Deployed Software Engineer

### The Mission

Chemist3 Labs does not build software in a vacuum. Our Forward Deployed Software Engineers operate at the bleeding edge of client integration — writing production-grade code inside live customer environments where failure is measured in operational downtime, not sprint velocity. You are the engineer who shows up, understands a client's technical landscape in days (not quarters), and ships working systems that solve problems no one else has been able to crack.

This role exists because the gap between "platform capability" and "client reality" is where most technology companies die. FDSEs are the bridge. You will adapt internal Chemist3 platforms, integrate third-party APIs, build custom tooling, and deliver systems that operate under real-world constraints — unreliable networks, legacy infrastructure, hostile data environments, and stakeholders who need results yesterday. If you need a product manager to tell you what to build, this role will break you. If you thrive when handed an ambiguous problem and a deadline, you will be dangerous here.

Average engineers write code. FDSEs at Chemist3 deliver outcomes. You will own your deployments end-to-end — from initial client discovery through architecture, implementation, testing, and production handoff. There is no "throw it over the wall." You are the wall.

### Core Responsibilities

- Deploy directly into client environments and deliver production-ready software integrations within compressed timelines, often under NDA and operational security constraints.
- Write, test, and ship backend and full-stack code that interfaces with customer data systems, internal APIs, and third-party services — with zero tolerance for fragile implementations.
- Conduct rapid technical discovery across unfamiliar codebases, legacy systems, and undocumented infrastructure to map integration surfaces and identify blockers.
- Architect bespoke adapter layers, ETL connectors, and middleware that translate between Chemist3 platform capabilities and client-specific data models and workflows.
- Own incident response and hotfix cycles for deployed systems — if it breaks in the field, you fix it in the field.
- Collaborate directly with client engineering teams, translating Chemist3's technical philosophy into joint development workflows without compromising system integrity.
- Maintain deployment documentation, integration runbooks, and post-engagement knowledge transfers that enable long-term client self-sufficiency.
- Feed field intelligence back to core engineering — bug reports, feature requests, and architectural insights drawn from real-world deployment friction.

### What We Look For

**Technical / Domain Competencies:**
- Strong proficiency in Python, TypeScript/JavaScript, or Go with production deployment experience.
- Deep familiarity with REST/GraphQL API design, integration patterns, and authentication protocols (OAuth2, SAML, mTLS).
- Experience operating in containerized environments (Docker, Kubernetes) and deploying to cloud providers (AWS, GCP, Azure) as well as on-premise infrastructure.
- Working knowledge of relational and non-relational databases, data serialization formats, and schema evolution strategies.
- Comfort with CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure-as-code, and automated testing frameworks in unfamiliar environments.
- Prior experience in consulting, defense-tech, or enterprise SaaS integration is a strong signal.

**Mindset & Attributes:**
- High tolerance for ambiguity — you can land in an unfamiliar environment and become productive within hours, not weeks.
- Direct, low-ego communication style — you tell clients and teammates what they need to hear, not what they want to hear.
- Rapid context-switching across multiple concurrent engagements without dropping quality.
- Deep personal accountability — you own outcomes, not tasks.
- Willingness to travel and operate in non-standard working environments, including client sites with restricted access.

### Operating Principles

- **Field-First Engineering:** The client environment is the source of truth. Code that works in staging but fails in the field is broken code.
- **Outcomes Over Deliverables:** We measure success by operational impact, not lines of code or tickets closed.
- **Zero Layering:** You talk directly to the people who matter — no intermediaries, no telephone games, no status reports that substitute for actual progress.
