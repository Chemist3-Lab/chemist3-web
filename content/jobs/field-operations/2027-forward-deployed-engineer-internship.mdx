# 2027 Forward Deployed Engineer Internship

### The Mission

Chemist3 Labs is pioneering novel computational and hardware-integrated platforms. As a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) Intern for Summer/Fall 2027, you will not be assigned to synthetic demo projects or internal toys. You will work alongside senior forward-deployed engineers directly on active projects, deploying software in close proximity to operational environments and partner laboratories.

This internship is designed for high-caliber students and early-career builders who want exposure to real systems engineering, edge deployments, and the intersection of software and physical operations. You will be expected to read production code on day one, take ownership of specific subsystem integrations, and bridge the gap between algorithmic prototypes and field-executable software.

### Core Responsibilities

- Collaborate directly with senior FDEs to build, integrate, and test software components across active research and development projects.
- Write robust, well-tested Python, Go, Rust, or TypeScript code deployed into live testbeds and staging environments.
- Develop diagnostic utilities, automated integration test harnesses, and data extraction pipelines that surface operational insights for engineering teams.
- Interface with laboratory hardware, sensor feeds, and telemetry streams to validate system reliability under real physical constraints.
- Participate in design reviews, technical architecture discussions, and daily operational standups.
- Document system interfaces, deployment procedures, and troubleshooting runbooks for project stakeholders.

### What We Look For

**Technical / Domain Competencies:**
- Enrolled in or recently completed a degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Chemical Engineering, or equivalent practical experience.
- Strong programming fundamentals in at least one modern language: Python, C/C++, Rust, Go, or TypeScript.
- Understanding of computer systems fundamentals: operating systems, processes, memory management, networking primitives (TCP/IP, HTTP, gRPC), and concurrency.
- Familiarity with Linux environments, command-line tooling, Git version control, and containerized development (Docker).
- Prior project experience building end-to-end software—demonstrated through open-source contributions, technical hackathons, robotics teams, or personal engineering builds.

**Mindset & Attributes:**
- High curiosity and extreme ownership: you seek to understand how entire systems work end-to-end rather than waiting for discrete instructions.
- Bias for action and rapid iteration: you test assumptions by running experiments and writing code.
- Strong communication skills: you can explain complex technical issues clearly and concisely.
- Low ego: you actively seek constructive code reviews and use feedback to improve your craft.

### Operating Principles

- **Direct Ownership:** Even as an intern, you own the code you ship and are accountable for its correctness and performance.
- **Physical Grounding:** Software must respect physical realities—latency, thermal boundaries, and real-world hardware limits.
- **Zero Abstraction Layering:** Work directly with senior engineers and operators without artificial administrative buffers.
