# Site Reliability Engineer

### The Mission

Chemist3 Labs deploys systems that clients depend on for operational decision-making. When those systems go down, the impact is not a degraded user experience — it is an operational blind spot. The Site Reliability Engineer ensures that Chemist3's platforms stay up, stay fast, and recover quickly when things go wrong. You are the engineer who builds the observability, fault tolerance, and incident response machinery that makes reliability a measurable, engineered property rather than a hope.

This role lives at the intersection of software engineering and operations. You will design telemetry systems that surface meaningful signals from distributed services, define and enforce error budgets that balance reliability with deployment velocity, build automated recovery mechanisms that handle failure faster than a human can respond, and lead incident response when automated systems are not enough. Your work directly determines whether Chemist3 can credibly promise uptime to clients who need it.

If your SRE experience consists of monitoring dashboards and paging on-call engineers, this role will demand more of you. If you have defined SLOs that changed how engineering teams prioritize work, built chaos engineering programs that uncovered real production weaknesses, and designed alerting systems that have a signal-to-noise ratio worth defending, you will thrive here.

### Core Responsibilities

- Define, implement, and enforce Service Level Objectives (SLOs) and error budgets across Chemist3 platform services — translating reliability requirements into quantitative targets that drive engineering prioritization.
- Design and operate observability infrastructure: metrics collection (Prometheus, VictoriaMetrics), distributed tracing (Jaeger, Tempo), structured logging (Loki, Elasticsearch), and unified dashboards (Grafana) that provide actionable visibility into system behavior.
- Build alerting systems with high signal-to-noise ratio: symptom-based alerts, multi-signal correlation, escalation policies, and runbook-linked notifications that enable rapid incident triage.
- Design fault-tolerant architectures: redundancy patterns, graceful degradation, circuit breakers, retry strategies, bulkheads, and failover mechanisms that limit blast radius when components fail.
- Lead incident response: real-time coordination during production incidents, systematic root cause analysis, blameless postmortems, and action item tracking that drives structural improvements.
- Develop and execute chaos engineering experiments: fault injection, latency simulation, dependency failure testing, and capacity stress tests that validate resilience assumptions under controlled conditions.
- Automate operational toil: self-healing systems, auto-scaling policies, certificate rotation, log rotation, and routine maintenance workflows that reduce human intervention for predictable operational tasks.
- Collaborate with Software Engineers to improve service reliability at the code level: connection pooling, timeout configuration, health check implementations, and dependency management practices.

### What We Look For

**Technical / Domain Competencies:**
- Strong software engineering skills in Python, Go, or equivalent — SRE at Chemist3 is an engineering role, not an operations role.
- Deep experience with observability systems: Prometheus, Grafana, Jaeger/Tempo, Loki/Elasticsearch, and OpenTelemetry instrumentation.
- Proficiency with Kubernetes operations: deployment strategies, resource management, pod disruption budgets, horizontal pod autoscaling, and cluster reliability patterns.
- Understanding of distributed systems failure modes: partial failures, split-brain scenarios, cascading failures, thundering herds, and consensus protocol limitations.
- Experience defining and operating SLO/SLI frameworks — including error budget policies that influence release cadence and engineering prioritization.
- Incident management experience: structured incident command, communication protocols, postmortem facilitation, and follow-up tracking.
- Familiarity with chaos engineering tools and methodologies (Litmus, Chaos Monkey, Gremlin, or custom fault injection).
- Linux performance analysis: CPU profiling, memory leak detection, I/O analysis, network diagnostics, and kernel-level troubleshooting.

**Mindset & Attributes:**
- Engineering discipline — you solve operational problems by writing code and building systems, not by adding manual processes and checklists.
- Data-driven reliability thinking — you measure everything, trust metrics over intuition, and make decisions based on error budgets rather than feelings about system stability.
- Calm under pressure — you lead incident response with clarity, structure, and composure, even when production is on fire.
- Proactive risk identification — you hunt for reliability weaknesses before they manifest as incidents, not after.
- Strong communication skills for incident coordination, postmortem writing, and cross-team collaboration on reliability improvements.

### Operating Principles

- **Outcomes Over Deliverables:** Achieving SLO targets and reducing incident frequency and duration are the metrics that matter. Dashboard count and alert rule count are not.
- **Extreme Ownership:** If a service you are responsible for goes down and recovery is slow, you own the gap — whether it was a missing alert, an untested failover, or an inadequate runbook.
- **Zero Layering:** SREs work directly with the engineers who write the services. Reliability is not an external review — it is an integrated engineering practice.
