# Enterprise Growth Strategist

### The Mission

Chemist3 Labs builds technology that solves hard problems for demanding clients. The Enterprise Growth Strategist is the operator who finds those clients, builds the relationships, structures the deals, and drives the revenue pipeline that funds everything else. You are not a "sales rep" with a quota and a script. You are a strategic operator who understands the technology deeply enough to identify where it creates value, navigates complex enterprise and government procurement processes, and closes deals that create genuine, long-term partnerships — not one-off transactions.

This role exists because Chemist3's go-to-market motion is not bottoms-up PLG or inbound marketing. Our clients are enterprise and government organizations with long sales cycles, complex procurement requirements, and high-stakes evaluation processes. Winning these deals requires someone who can build trust with senior technical and executive stakeholders, credibly discuss platform capabilities without engineering support on every call, and structure commercial terms that align Chemist3's success with client outcomes.

If your sales experience is limited to transactional SaaS deals with self-serve onboarding, this role operates at a fundamentally different level. If you have built relationships with defense program managers, enterprise CTOs, or government contracting officers — and can point to deals you sourced, structured, and closed — you understand what this requires.

### Core Responsibilities

- Build and manage Chemist3's enterprise pipeline: identifying target accounts, mapping organizational decision structures, developing engagement strategies, and maintaining CRM discipline throughout multi-month sales cycles.
- Conduct strategic prospecting: attending industry events, leveraging network relationships, executing targeted outreach campaigns, and building referral channels within defense, intelligence, and enterprise verticals.
- Lead technical discovery and qualification conversations with prospective clients — understanding their operational challenges, data environments, and technical requirements well enough to articulate how Chemist3's platform creates measurable value.
- Structure commercial proposals: pricing models, pilot program frameworks, licensing terms, statement of work scopes, and partnership structures that align incentives and manage risk for both Chemist3 and the client.
- Navigate enterprise and government procurement processes: RFP responses, compliance documentation, security questionnaires, and multi-stakeholder approval chains.
- Negotiate contract terms in collaboration with Legal & Commercial Counsel — balancing revenue optimization with relationship preservation and IP protection.
- Coordinate post-sale handoffs to Deployment Strategists and Forward Deployed teams — ensuring that commercial commitments translate into technically executable engagement plans.
- Report pipeline metrics, forecast revenue, and provide market intelligence to leadership — grounding strategic decisions in quantitative pipeline data and competitive positioning.

### What We Look For

**Technical / Domain Competencies:**
- Sufficient technical fluency to discuss data infrastructure, API integrations, deployment architectures, and security requirements with technical buyers — without requiring an engineer in every meeting.
- Experience selling complex technology solutions to enterprise and/or government clients with sales cycles exceeding 3 months and deal sizes exceeding six figures.
- Familiarity with defense and intelligence procurement: FedRAMP, ITAR considerations, SBIR/STTR programs, OTA contracts, and the general mechanics of government technology acquisition.
- Strong commercial structuring skills: pricing models (per-seat, platform fee, outcome-based), pilot program design, and contract negotiation frameworks.
- CRM proficiency (Salesforce, HubSpot, Attio) and pipeline management discipline — forecasting accuracy matters.
- Excellent written and verbal communication: proposals, executive briefings, RFP responses, and follow-up correspondence that are clear, precise, and compelling.

**Mindset & Attributes:**
- Relationship-driven selling — you build trust through competence and follow-through, not through charm or pressure.
- Strategic patience combined with tactical urgency — you understand that enterprise deals take time but you drive momentum relentlessly within each cycle.
- Intellectual curiosity about the technology and the client's domain — you genuinely want to understand the problem, not just close the deal.
- Resilience — you handle rejection, deal slippage, and procurement delays without losing energy or conviction.
- Ethical selling — you do not oversell capabilities or make commitments the engineering team cannot deliver. Your credibility is your most valuable asset.

### Operating Principles

- **Outcomes Over Deliverables:** Revenue closed on terms that create healthy, expandable client relationships is success. Activity metrics (calls made, emails sent) are inputs, not outcomes.
- **Zero Layering:** You communicate directly with engineering, product, and leadership. There is no "sales operations" buffer between you and the information you need to close deals.
- **Field-First Engineering:** The best sales conversations happen at client sites, industry events, and operational environments — not in email threads. Be present where the decisions are made.
