# Legal & Commercial Counsel

### The Mission

Chemist3 Labs operates at the intersection of defense technology, enterprise software, and sensitive data — a domain where a single poorly drafted contract clause, an overlooked export control regulation, or an inadequate IP protection strategy can create existential risk. The Legal & Commercial Counsel is the operator who ensures that Chemist3's commercial relationships, intellectual property, and compliance posture are structured to protect the company and enable aggressive business execution simultaneously.

This is not in-house legal for a company that needs someone to review NDAs and manage outside counsel. You will be structuring enterprise contracts with defense and intelligence clients, navigating ITAR and EAR export control frameworks, protecting proprietary technology through patent and trade secret strategies, negotiating pilot agreements and licensing terms that balance revenue with IP exposure, and building the compliance infrastructure that allows Chemist3 to operate in regulated markets without slowing down.

If your legal experience is limited to standard SaaS terms of service and cookie policies, this role operates in a different league. If you have structured deals in defense-tech, government contracting, or enterprise infrastructure — and can draft a contract that protects Chemist3's interests while closing the deal — you understand the dual mandate of this role.

### Core Responsibilities

- Draft, negotiate, and manage enterprise contracts: master service agreements, statements of work, data processing agreements, licensing terms, and pilot program structures tailored to defense, intelligence, and enterprise clients.
- Build and maintain Chemist3's IP protection strategy: patent portfolio planning, trade secret identification and protection protocols, invention disclosure processes, and open-source licensing compliance.
- Manage NDA and confidentiality frameworks across client engagements, partnerships, and employment relationships — with particular attention to the sensitivity levels common in defense and intelligence work.
- Navigate regulatory and compliance requirements: ITAR/EAR export controls, CFIUS considerations, data protection regulations (GDPR, CCPA), and government contracting regulations (FAR/DFARS) as applicable.
- Structure equity, investment, and corporate governance documents in coordination with outside counsel — cap table management, board resolutions, investor rights agreements, and financing instruments.
- Develop template libraries and playbooks for recurring legal workflows: standard contract templates, negotiation playbooks, risk assessment checklists, and approval workflows that enable business teams to move fast with appropriate guardrails.
- Advise leadership on legal risk across business decisions: market entry strategies, partnership structures, acquisition due diligence, and organizational changes.
- Manage relationships with outside counsel for specialized matters (patent prosecution, litigation, regulatory inquiries) — providing clear briefs and maintaining cost discipline.

### What We Look For

**Technical / Domain Competencies:**
- JD from an accredited law school with bar admission in at least one US jurisdiction.
- 3+ years of legal experience in technology transactions, IP, or commercial law — with preference for defense-tech, government contracting, or enterprise infrastructure sectors.
- Strong contract drafting and negotiation skills with a demonstrated ability to close complex deals without unnecessary legal friction.
- Working knowledge of IP law: patent prosecution and strategy, trade secret protection, copyright, and open-source licensing (GPL, Apache, MIT and their interaction with proprietary code).
- Familiarity with export control regimes (ITAR, EAR) and government contracting frameworks (FAR/DFARS) — or demonstrated ability to learn complex regulatory frameworks rapidly.
- Understanding of corporate governance, equity structures, and startup financing instruments (SAFEs, convertible notes, priced rounds).
- Experience building legal process automation: template libraries, contract management systems, and approval workflows.

**Mindset & Attributes:**
- Business-enabling orientation — you view your role as removing legal barriers to execution, not creating them. You find ways to say "yes, if" rather than "no."
- Comfort with ambiguity and imperfect information — you can provide actionable guidance based on available facts without requiring certainty before advising.
- Direct communication — you explain legal risk in plain language that non-lawyers can act on, without hedging everything into meaninglessness.
- Speed — you can turn around contract reviews, risk assessments, and legal opinions at the velocity a startup requires, not at the pace of a large law firm.
- Discretion — you will handle the most sensitive commercial, personnel, and strategic information in the organization.

### Operating Principles

- **Outcomes Over Deliverables:** A deal that closes on terms that protect Chemist3 and satisfy the client is success. A perfect contract that kills the deal through excessive negotiation is failure.
- **Zero Layering:** Legal advice goes directly to the decision-maker. There is no chain of intermediaries between your analysis and the business decision.
- **Extreme Ownership:** If a contract exposes Chemist3 to avoidable risk, or a compliance gap creates a regulatory issue, you own the failure — regardless of whether someone else signed the document.
