# Operations & People Lead

### The Mission

Chemist3 Labs is a high-intensity, mission-driven organization that runs on operational discipline, not corporate process. The Operations & People Lead is the person who builds and maintains the internal operating rhythm that allows engineers, field operators, and business teams to move fast without the organization collapsing under its own growth. You own the cadences, systems, and people operations that keep Chemist3 functioning as a cohesive unit rather than a collection of autonomous individuals.

This is not a traditional HR role. You will not be writing employee handbook policies for a Fortune 500 company. You will be building the operational infrastructure of a fast-moving defense-tech startup: hiring pipelines that source exceptional talent without bureaucratic drag, onboarding systems that make new operators productive in days, internal communication rhythms that keep distributed teams aligned, and people processes that are as lean and effective as the engineering systems they support.

If your experience is in corporate HR departments where compliance and risk avoidance are the primary objectives, this role will feel alien. If you have built operational systems in high-growth startups, military units, or mission-driven organizations where speed, clarity, and accountability are the operating principles, you will recognize what we are building.

### Core Responsibilities

- Design and operate internal operating cadences: weekly syncs, monthly reviews, quarterly planning cycles, and annual retrospectives — structured for maximum information density and minimum meeting overhead.
- Build and manage the talent pipeline: job postings, sourcing strategies, screening processes, interview frameworks, and offer workflows calibrated for the technical and cultural intensity Chemist3 demands.
- Own the onboarding experience: system access provisioning, security clearance coordination, team introductions, operational orientation, and 30/60/90-day integration checkpoints that ensure new hires are productive quickly.
- Manage people operations: compensation administration, benefits coordination, performance review frameworks, promotion processes, and offboarding procedures.
- Develop and maintain internal communication systems: knowledge bases, org-wide announcements, team updates, and information architecture that keeps everyone informed without creating notification overload.
- Track and manage organizational health metrics: headcount planning, retention analysis, engagement indicators, and capacity forecasting to support leadership decision-making.
- Coordinate office and lab logistics: workspace planning, equipment procurement, vendor management for office services, and physical security coordination.
- Serve as the operational backstop for cross-functional coordination — ensuring that commitments between teams are tracked, blockers are surfaced, and organizational friction is identified and resolved.

### What We Look For

**Technical / Domain Competencies:**
- Experience building operations and people systems in startups or high-growth technical organizations (Series A through Series C scale).
- Proficiency with operational tooling: project management (Linear, Notion, Asana), HRIS (Rippling, Gusto, BambooHR), applicant tracking (Lever, Greenhouse, Ashby), and communication platforms (Slack, Discord).
- Understanding of compensation structures in technical organizations: salary bands, equity packages, and competitive positioning analysis.
- Familiarity with employment law fundamentals relevant to the operating jurisdictions — enough to manage compliance without requiring legal review for routine decisions.
- Strong written communication for internal announcements, process documentation, and organizational communications.
- Experience with security clearance processes and government compliance requirements is a plus.

**Mindset & Attributes:**
- Operational intensity — you treat internal process with the same rigor and urgency that engineering teams apply to production systems.
- Low-ego service orientation — your success is measured by how effectively other people can do their jobs, not by the size of your team or budget.
- Direct communication — you give candid feedback to leadership and team members without political hedging.
- Systems thinking applied to organizational design — you see patterns, bottlenecks, and leverage points in how information and decisions flow through the organization.
- High discretion and trustworthiness — you will handle sensitive personnel, compensation, and organizational information with absolute confidentiality.

### Operating Principles

- **Zero Layering:** People operations are direct. Feedback flows without intermediaries. Hiring decisions are made by the people who will work with the candidate, not by an HR committee.
- **Outcomes Over Deliverables:** A lean onboarding process that makes people productive in a week is better than a comprehensive onboarding program that takes a month. Measure what matters.
- **Extreme Ownership:** If a new hire fails because the onboarding was inadequate, or a critical role stays unfilled because the pipeline was neglected, you own that failure.
