# Finance & Resource Controller

### The Mission

Chemist3 Labs operates with finite capital, aggressive timelines, and zero tolerance for financial surprises. The Finance & Resource Controller is the operator who ensures that every dollar is tracked, every expenditure is justified, and leadership has the financial visibility required to make decisions that keep the company funded, compliant, and strategically positioned. You are not a bookkeeper processing invoices — you are the financial operator who controls burn rate, manages runway, and provides the analytical foundation for resource allocation decisions.

This role matters because startups do not die from bad technology — they die from running out of money. You will manage the financial infrastructure that prevents that: accurate and timely books, cash flow forecasting, vendor management, invoicing and collections, payroll coordination, and the financial modeling that informs fundraising strategy and budget allocation. You will be the person who tells leadership the truth about the numbers, even when the truth is uncomfortable.

If your finance experience is limited to large corporate environments with armies of analysts and established ERP systems, the resource constraints and speed requirements of a Series A startup will test you. If you have managed finance for early-stage companies where you were the entire finance function — building the systems, running the processes, and producing the analysis — you understand what this demands.

### Core Responsibilities

- Maintain accurate and timely financial records: general ledger, accounts payable/receivable, bank reconciliations, and month-end close procedures that produce reliable financial statements (income statement, balance sheet, cash flow).
- Build and maintain financial models: burn rate analysis, runway projections, scenario planning, and budget-vs-actual tracking that provide leadership with decision-grade financial visibility.
- Manage cash flow operations: invoice generation and collections, vendor payment scheduling, expense approval workflows, and working capital optimization.
- Coordinate payroll processing, contractor payments, benefits administration, and equity-related financial transactions in collaboration with the Operations & People Lead.
- Own vendor management: contract review for financial terms, procurement approvals, subscription tracking, and cost optimization across SaaS tools, cloud infrastructure, hardware, and professional services.
- Prepare financial materials for fundraising: investor decks, financial data rooms, due diligence responses, and cap table management in coordination with Legal & Commercial Counsel.
- Manage tax compliance: quarterly estimated payments, annual filings, R&D tax credit documentation, sales tax obligations, and coordination with external tax advisors and CPAs.
- Implement and maintain financial systems and controls: accounting software (QuickBooks, Xero, or equivalent), expense management platforms, approval hierarchies, and internal controls appropriate for the company's stage and regulatory requirements.

### What We Look For

**Technical / Domain Competencies:**
- Strong foundation in accounting principles (GAAP) and financial statement preparation — CPA, CMA, or equivalent credential is valued but not required if compensated by demonstrated competence.
- Experience managing finance for early-stage technology companies (Seed through Series B) — including the unique challenges of equity accounting, revenue recognition for enterprise contracts, and fundraising financial preparation.
- Proficiency with accounting software (QuickBooks Online, Xero, NetSuite) and financial modeling tools (Excel/Google Sheets at an advanced level, with experience building multi-scenario financial models).
- Working knowledge of startup financial structures: SAFEs, convertible notes, priced rounds, 409A valuations, stock option accounting, and cap table management.
- Familiarity with government contracting financial requirements (DCAA compliance, cost accounting standards) is a strong signal given Chemist3's defense-tech positioning.
- Experience with expense management, corporate card programs, and procurement workflows at startup scale.
- Understanding of tax obligations for technology startups: R&D tax credits, state nexus analysis, international contractor payments, and transfer pricing basics.

**Mindset & Attributes:**
- Operational rigor — you close the books on time, every time. Financial discipline is non-negotiable.
- Analytical clarity — you present financial data in ways that enable decisions, not in ways that require a finance degree to interpret.
- Proactive risk identification — you flag cash flow concerns, budget overruns, and compliance gaps before they become crises.
- Frugality orientation — you treat company capital with the same discipline you would apply to your own money.
- Discretion — you will have visibility into the most sensitive financial, compensation, and strategic information in the organization.
- Speed — you operate at startup velocity, producing analysis and reports in hours or days, not weeks.

### Operating Principles

- **Extreme Ownership:** If the books are late, if a tax filing is missed, or if leadership is surprised by a cash flow problem, you own the failure. Financial operations are your domain and your responsibility.
- **Outcomes Over Deliverables:** Financial visibility that enables better decisions is success. Producing reports that no one reads or understands is waste.
- **Zero Layering:** Financial analysis goes directly to the decision-makers. There is no chain of intermediaries between the numbers and the people who act on them.
