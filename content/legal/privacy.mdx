## 1. Regulatory Context & Scope of Notice

Chemist3 Labs ("**Chemist3 Labs**", "**Company**", "**we**", "**us**", or "**our**") engineers mission-critical computational intelligence, autonomous chemical simulation platforms, and enterprise-grade decision systems. We maintain rigorous standards of operational integrity, information security, and global data privacy compliance.

This Enterprise Privacy Policy and Data Protection Notice ("**Privacy Policy**" or "**Notice**") governs the collection, categorization, processing, storage, and cross-border transfer of personal information collected through:

1. **Digital Properties and Public Touchpoints:** Our primary web platform, developer portals, research archives, public API landing interfaces, and technical documentation repositories;
2. **Direct Communications and Enterprise Outreach:** Corporate correspondence, technical inquiries, request-for-proposals (RFPs), and prospective client interactions routed to our operational desks, including correspondence addressed to [`legal@chemist3.com`](mailto:legal@chemist3.com); and
3. **Enterprise Evaluation Instances:** Pre-production demonstrations, sandbox environments, and developer access channels.

### 1.1 Data Controller vs. Data Processor Dichotomy

To ensure structural clarity under international data protection frameworks—including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) ("**GDPR**"), the UK Data Protection Act 2018 ("**UK GDPR**"), and the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act ("**CCPA/CPRA**"):

* **Chemist3 Labs as a Data Controller:** Chemist3 Labs operates as an independent Data Controller with respect to personal data collected directly from visitors to our public websites, participants in our direct correspondence channels, business development contacts, and technical telemetry generated across our public infrastructures.
* **Chemist3 Labs as a Data Processor / Service Provider:** With respect to mission-critical operational inputs, proprietary customer datasets, proprietary chemical libraries, and enterprise client workloads ingested into our production environments ("**Client Operational Data**"), Chemist3 Labs processes such data strictly as a Data Processor (or Service Provider) pursuant to executed Master Services Agreements ("**MSAs**"), Data Processing Addenda ("**DPAs**"), and bilateral confidentiality instruments. Client Operational Data is governed exclusively by the applicable enterprise agreement and is subject to the zero secondary training covenants set forth in Section 3 of this Notice.

## 3. Data Governance, Isolation & Zero Secondary Training Covenants

As a provider of advanced intelligence systems operating in sensitive, proprietary, and mission-critical domains, Chemist3 Labs maintains an uncompromising data governance and security posture.

### 3.1 Strict "Zero Secondary Training" Commitment

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                        ZERO SECONDARY TRAINING COVENANT                           |
|                                                                                   |
|  Chemist3 Labs DOES NOT ingest, exploit, or repurpose proprietary Client          |
|  Operational Data (Tier C) or Direct Inquiries (Tier B) to pre-train, fine-tune,  |
|  align, or reinforce any generalized foundation models, publicly shared weights,  |
|  or third-party algorithms without express, bilateral, executed authorization.    |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

1. **Model Isolation:** Client-specific adaptations, vector embeddings, fine-tuned checkpoints, and algorithmic inference pipelines exist solely within cryptographically isolated, tenant-specific boundary layers.
2. **No Data Leakage Across Tenants:** Client data is structurally insulated. Algorithmic outputs, synthesis pathways, and molecular predictions generated for Client A cannot permeate, inform, or serve as inference priors for Client B.
3. **No Commercial Brokerage:** Chemist3 Labs does not sell, lease, rent, barter, or distribute personal information or proprietary client data to data aggregators, ad exchanges, or commercial brokers.

### 3.2 Tenant Isolation and Logical Architecture

Chemist3 Labs operationalizes infrastructure security through modern defense-in-depth principles:

* **Multi-Tier Segmentation:** Production compute pipelines, storage volumes, and telemetry services reside in isolated virtual networks (VPCs) with zero direct routing between disparate customer namespaces.
* **Cryptographic Tenancy:** Customer data stores employ client-isolated encryption key sets managed through Hardware Security Modules (HSM) conforming to FIPS 140-2 Level 3 or higher.

### 3.3 Data Retention & Cryptographic Disposal Protocols

* **Telemetry Retention:** Tier A diagnostic logs and network telemetry are retained for an active lifecycle not exceeding ninety (90) days, after which they are either irreversibly aggregated into statistical summaries or purged via automated lifecycle management policies.
* **Correspondence Retention:** Tier B correspondence directed to [`legal@chemist3.com`](mailto:legal@chemist3.com) is preserved only for the duration of the operational evaluation, procurement cycle, or statutory recordkeeping mandates (typically three (3) to five (5) years under commercial dispute limitations), after which records are purged.
* **Operational Data Purging:** Tier C operational data is eradicated upon contract termination, customer instruction, or mutual schedule expiration using automated DoD 5220.22-M / NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 1 cryptographic erasure standards.

## 4. Technical & Organizational Security Measures (TOMs)

Chemist3 Labs designs its infrastructure around the presumption of adversarial threat models. We implement industry-leading technical and organizational measures to preserve the confidentiality, integrity, availability, and resilience of all processing environments.

### 4.1 Cryptographic Standards

* **Data in Transit:** All external and inter-service communications mandate Transport Layer Security (TLS) 1.3 (or strictly configured TLS 1.2 fallbacks) utilizing robust cryptographic cipher suites (e.g., ECDHE-RSA-AES256-GCM-SHA384, ChaCha20-Poly1305) with Perfect Forward Secrecy (PFS) enforced. Unencrypted HTTP endpoints are categorically denied or redirected with HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS) preloaded headers.
* **Data at Rest:** All persistence volumes, object stores, database backups, and ephemeral caches are encrypted utilizing Advanced Encryption Standard with 256-bit keys (AES-256) or equivalent standardized authenticated ciphers.

### 4.2 Identity & Access Governance

* **Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP):** Access to internal staging, monitoring dashboards, and production compute substrates is strictly restricted according to fine-grained Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) matrices.
* **Mandatory Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** Access to corporate systems, source repositories, and operational infrastructure requires hardware-backed FIDO2/WebAuthn tokens or time-based one-time password (TOTP) authenticators.
* **Immutable Audit Logging:** System engineer interactions, administrative actions, and privileged commands are streamed in near-real-time to immutable, append-only, tamper-evident log stores subject to continuous anomaly detection.

### 4.3 Incident Response and Breach Notification

Chemist3 Labs maintains a rigorous Security Incident Response Plan (SIRP). In the event of a confirmed security incident resulting in unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to personal data:

1. **Forensic Containment:** Immediate revocation of compromised credentials, isolation of affected compute nodes, and initiation of root-cause forensic analysis.
2. **Regulatory & Client Escalation:** Notice to competent supervisory authorities and impacted enterprise clients without undue delay, and where feasible, within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of a qualifying personal data breach, pursuant to Article 33 of the GDPR and applicable state regulations.

## 7. Data Subject Rights & Regulatory Disclosures

Chemist3 Labs guarantees comprehensive rights to data subjects whose personal data we process as a Data Controller.

### 7.1 Rights Under GDPR / UK GDPR

Individuals residing within the EEA or the United Kingdom possess the following statutory rights under Articles 15–22 of the GDPR/UK GDPR:

* **Right of Access (Article 15):** The right to obtain confirmation as to whether personal data concerning you is being processed, and to receive a copy of such data along with detailed processing disclosures.
* **Right to Rectification (Article 16):** The right to obtain without undue delay the rectification of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
* **Right to Erasure / Right to be Forgotten (Article 17):** The right to request the permanent erasure of personal data under qualifying legal grounds (e.g., data is no longer necessary for the purposes collected).
* **Right to Restriction of Processing (Article 18):** The right to restrict processing during ongoing verification of accuracy, legal claims, or legitimate interest balancing assessments.
* **Right to Data Portability (Article 20):** The right to receive personal data you have provided to us in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
* **Right to Object (Article 21):** The right to object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to the processing of your personal data predicated upon our legitimate interests.
* **Rights Regarding Automated Decision-Making (Article 22):** Chemist3 Labs does not subject data subjects to legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing or profiling.

### 7.2 Disclosures for California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020 (collectively, "**CCPA/CPRA**"), California residents are entitled to specific disclosures:

1. **Categories of Personal Information Collected:** Within the preceding twelve (12) months, Chemist3 Labs has collected identifiers (IP addresses, contact emails), internet/network activity telemetry, and professional or employment-related information (business correspondence).
2. **Statutory Sale & Sharing Prohibition:** Chemist3 Labs **does not sell** your personal information, nor do we **share** personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We have had no actual knowledge of selling or sharing personal information of consumers under 16 years of age.
3. **Use of Sensitive Personal Information:** We do not collect or process Sensitive Personal Information (as defined under CPRA Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.140(ae)) for the purpose of inferring characteristics about individuals.
4. **Consumer Rights:** California consumers maintain the Right to Know, Right to Delete, Right to Correct, and the Right to Non-Discrimination for exercising statutory privacy rights.

### 7.3 Exercise of Rights & Verification Protocol

To exercise any of the statutory rights enumerated above, you or your authorized legal representative may transmit a formal, authenticated request to our Legal Operations Desk:

* **Email:** [`legal@chemist3.com`](mailto:legal@chemist3.com)
* **Subject Header Convention:** `LEGAL PRIVACY DEMAND - [FULL NAME / ENTITY]`

**Verification Standard:** To prevent unauthorized disclosure or compromise of data, Chemist3 Labs applies strict identity verification protocols. We may require proof of identity, matching email confirmation from the registered address, or corporate authorization credentials before processing any demand. We respond to all verified requests within thirty (30) days (or within statutory extension windows where permitted by complex requests).
